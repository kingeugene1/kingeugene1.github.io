Research

Connecting structural mechanics, vibration sensing and computational models.

01 · Current research

Seismic structural health monitoring

Multi-floor motion measurements and finite element simulations for understanding earthquake response and assessing structural damage.

02 · Doctoral research

Crowdsourced vibration sensing

Using vibration measurements collected from multiple vehicles to support scalable bridge structural health monitoring.

03 · Research foundation

Vehicle–bridge interaction

Numerical modelling of coupled vehicle and bridge dynamics, with emphasis on system identification and extending one-dimensional models toward three-dimensional representations.

04 · Research foundation

Drive-by bridge monitoring

Bridge vibration and modal identification using vehicle-mounted sensors and synchronized vehicle–bridge data.

Research approach

01

Measure

Collect and synchronize structural or vehicle vibration observations.

02

Model

Represent dynamic responses using numerical structural models.

03

Identify

Compare measurements and predictions to estimate structural properties.

04

Assess

Evaluate the evidence and uncertainty relevant to infrastructure condition.
