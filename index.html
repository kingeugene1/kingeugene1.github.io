Structural engineering · Tsukuba, Japan

Dr. Eugene Mudahemuka

Postdoctoral researcher in structural dynamics and health monitoring.

I study how vibration measurements and computational models can reveal the condition of buildings and bridges. My current work at the University of Tsukuba focuses on earthquake response and structural damage assessment.

Research focus

01 · Current research

Seismic structural health monitoring

Multi-floor motion measurements and finite element simulations for understanding earthquake response and assessing structural damage.

02 · Doctoral research

Vehicle–bridge interaction

Numerical modelling of coupled vehicle and bridge dynamics, with emphasis on system identification and extending one-dimensional models toward three-dimensional representations.

About

I completed my Ph.D. in Engineering Mechanics and Energy at the University of Tsukuba on 25 September 2026. My doctoral work focused on drive-by bridge monitoring and vehicle–bridge interaction.
