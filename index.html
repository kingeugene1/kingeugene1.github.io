01 / About

Research focus

My research focuses on drive-by bridge monitoring, which uses vibrations recorded by sensors on passing vehicles to infer how a bridge responds. I study how vehicles and bridges interact, and how to extract bridge properties such as natural frequencies from vibration data.

I have a background in civil engineering and use numerical modeling, signal processing, and Python for research and data analysis.

  • Bridge health monitoring
  • Vehicle–bridge interaction
  • Vibration analysis
