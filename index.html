About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Engineering Mechanics and Energy at the University of Tsukuba. I compare motion recorded by sensors on multiple floors with finite element simulations to study how buildings respond to earthquakes and develop methods for assessing structural damage.

I completed my Ph.D. in Engineering Mechanics and Energy at the University of Tsukuba on 25 September 2026. My doctoral research focused on drive-by bridge monitoring and vehicle–bridge interaction.
