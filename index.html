About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Engineering Mechanics and Energy at the University of Tsukuba. My research focuses on drive-by bridge monitoring, which uses vibrations recorded by sensors on passing vehicles to help assess bridge condition. I study how vehicles and bridges interact and use numerical modeling, signal processing, and Python to analyze vibration data.
